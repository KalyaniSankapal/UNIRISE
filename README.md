# UNIRISE

## Connect. Discover. Rise.

UNIRISE is a campus-focused digital platform designed to connect students, clubs, coordinators and college administrators in one place.

The platform provides a space for students to discover campus events, hackathons, internships, clubs and student activities while also enabling communication and networking within the campus community.

---

## Problem Statement

Students often depend on multiple unofficial platforms and communication groups to discover campus opportunities, communicate with other students, find clubs and participate in events.

Important information about hackathons, internships, events and student activities can easily get lost in different communication channels.

UNIRISE aims to bring these campus activities into one organized platform.

---

## Our Solution

UNIRISE provides a unified campus platform with:

- Campus event feed
- Hackathon opportunities
- Internship opportunities
- Student posts
- Club posts
- Campus clubs directory
- Open communication
- Community groups
- Personal chats
- Student profiles
- Club verification
- Club coordinator management
- Notifications
- AI-powered search and recommendations

---

## Main Features

### 1. Campus Feed

Students can discover:

- Events
- Hackathons
- Internships
- Club activities
- Student posts
- Announcements

Posts can be categorized using hashtags and categories.

---

### 2. Events

Students can browse campus events and open a post to view complete information such as:

- Event description
- Date
- Time
- Location
- Registration links
- Attachments

---

### 3. Hackathons

Students can discover hackathons and access application or registration links.

---

### 4. Internship Opportunities

Students can discover internship opportunities and access application links and deadlines.

---

### 5. Communication

UNIRISE provides:

- Open campus communication
- Community groups
- Personal chats

The communication interface is designed to provide a familiar messaging experience.

---

### 6. Search

Users can search for:

- Students
- Clubs
- Events
- Hackathons
- Internships
- Communication groups
- Posts

The platform is designed to provide AI-assisted search suggestions and recommendations.

---

### 7. Clubs

Students can discover official college clubs through a dedicated clubs directory.

Club pages can contain:

- Club name
- Logo
- Tagline
- Cover image
- Description
- Posts
- Events
- Core team
- Club positions

---

### 8. Verified Club Positions

UNIRISE connects student profiles with their official club positions.

For example:

- President
- Vice President
- Secretary
- Treasurer
- Coordinator

Club positions can be transferred to another verified member when required.

---

### 9. Student Profiles

Students can create profiles containing:

- Profile photo
- Department
- Year
- Skills
- Achievements
- Club associations
- Campus positions
- Posts

---

### 10. Notifications

Users can receive notifications about:

- Clubs
- Groups
- Events
- Hackathons
- Internships
- Campus activities

---

### 11. Role-Based Access

UNIRISE supports different types of users:

- Student
- Club Coordinator
- College Administrator

Different roles can access different features.
